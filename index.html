Reading: Model Essay

Has technology made a positive contribution to sport today?

The role of technology in modern sport is a topic of much debate. While some argue it detracts from the human element of sport, I believe that technology has made a largely positive contribution to sport today.

Firstly, technology has significantly improved athletic performance. Advanced training equipment, wearable devices, and data analytics allow athletes to monitor their progress and optimise their training. This has led to faster times, higher jumps, and better overall results.

Secondly, technology has enhanced refereeing decisions. Systems like VAR in football and Hawk-Eye in tennis have reduced human error and ensured fairer outcomes. Although these systems are not perfect, they have made matches more just and transparent.

Finally, technology has improved the spectator experience. High-definition broadcasts, instant replays, and virtual reality have brought fans closer to the action. This has increased engagement and made sport more accessible to a global audience.

In conclusion, while technology presents some challenges, its benefits to performance, fairness, and fan engagement are clear. I believe that technology has indeed made a positive contribution to sport today.

Slide 5

Essay Planning

How to go about it:

  1. Plan: For each of the three points, make a list of relevant ideas.
  2. Select: From your lists, choose the points you want to include.
  3. Develop: Make notes, developing your ideas with reasons and examples.
  4. Organise: Decide how you will introduce and conclude your essay.
  5. Write: Use paragraphs and linking devices. Write in a formal/neutral style.
  6. Check: Review grammar, vocabulary, spelling and punctuation.

Remember: A balanced essay presents both sides of the argument before reaching a conclusion.

Review: Comparisons and Articles

Comparative Structures

  • One syllable: fast → faster
  • Two syllables (-y): heavy → heavier
  • Two+ syllables: expensive → more expensive
  • Irregular: good → better
  • as...as: as fast as
  • the + comparative, the + comparative: the more, the better

Article Rules

  • a/an: singular countable nouns (first mention)
  • the: specific nouns, superlatives, unique things
  • (no article): plural/general nouns, abstract nouns, meals, most place names

Quick Practice: Correct these sentences:

  • She is more taller than me. → __________
  • I have a apple. → __________

Technology and Change Part 2 - Teacher Notes

Aims

  • Analyse essay structure and use of linking devices
  • Review comparative structures and articles
  • Practise key word transformation and word formation
  • Prepare for and complete B2 First essay on technology in sport

Timing

120 minutes (2 hours)

Procedure

  1. Introduction and Model Essay (12 min): Read and analyse the model essay. Discuss the questions.
  2. Linking Devices (10 min): Complete the table and add the linking devices.
  3. Speaking Part 3 (10 min): Discuss the internet and enjoyment of life. Brainstorm ideas.
  4. Essay Planning (8 min): Review the planning steps.
  5. Comparisons Review (10 min): Complete the gap-fill and check answers.
  6. Articles Review (10 min): Correct the article mistakes.
  7. Key Word Transformation (10 min): Complete the sentences.
  8. Word Formation (10 min): Form the nouns from the given words.
  9. Writing (20 min): Students write the essay on the linked page.
  10. Speaking Part 4 (10 min): Discuss the questions in pairs.

Answers

Comparisons Review (Slide 6): 1-the, 2-most, 3-in, 4-many, 5-much, 6-so, 7-lot, 8-nearly, 9-by, 10-less

Articles Review (Slide 7): 3-delete "a", 4-add "the" before "way", 5-add "a" before "better", 6-change "a" to "the", 7-delete "the" before "life", 8-delete "the" before "more", 9-change "a" to "the", 10-delete "the" before "many", 12-delete "the" before "conclusion", 13-add "The" before "older", delete "of" before "effort"

Key Word Transformation (Slide 8): 1-is much bigger than, 2-are not as many, 3-the more/the less, 4-is the same height as, 5-has made more of

Word Formation (Slide 9): 1-EARLIEST, 2-INVENTOR, 3-RESEARCHERS, 4-EXISTENCE, 5-EQUIPMENT, 6-COLLECTION, 7-ASSISTANT, 8-RESPONSIBILITIES

Checking Questions

  • What makes a good essay introduction?
  • How do linking devices improve an essay?
  • When do we use "the" vs "a/an"?
  • How do we form comparative adjectives?

Likely Errors

  • Using "more better" instead of "better"
  • Omitting articles or using the wrong one
  • Using informal language in essays
  • Forgetting to address all three points

Extension Ideas

  • Students write a second essay on a different topic
  • Peer review of essays using the checklist
  • Debate: Is technology in sport a good thing?
